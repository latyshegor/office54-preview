Frequently asked questions

Your question is not here? Message us on WhatsApp or Telegram.

Is applying free of charge?

Yes. Applying and placement are free for applicants.

Do I need to speak German?

For most jobs, no. In order picking in Oldenburg, Pick-by-Voice also runs in English, Russian or Polish; you only need to know the numbers from 1 to 100 in German. Mechanics need German at B1 or higher. Each job says what is required.

How much will I earn?

Hourly wages range from €15.33 to €19.00 gross. On top come the GVP collective-agreement premiums: night work +25 %, Sunday work +50 %, public holiday work +100 %, overtime +25 %. Order picking in Oldenburg also pays a performance bonus.

Is accommodation available?

Yes, if needed, for €390 per person per month. For many jobs we organise the ride to work for €5 per working day, there and back.

In which languages can I get advice?

In German, English, Russian, Ukrainian, Polish and Romanian.

How are the jobs checked?

Our team checks every job posting before it goes online. We only work with employers we know.

Can I get notified about new jobs?

There are no automatic notifications at the moment. New jobs always appear on this page.
