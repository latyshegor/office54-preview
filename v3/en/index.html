Together with our partner company Caplex GmbH, we prepare applicants for their assignment in a workshop. In a practical welding test, we check existing MIG/MAG welding skills and refresh them where needed. This lets applicants show what they can do, regain confidence and get familiar with the requirements of their future workplace.
