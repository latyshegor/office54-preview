Frequently asked questions

Your question is not here? Write to us on WhatsApp or Telegram.

Can I work in Germany without speaking German?

Yes. Most of our jobs do not require German: bakery production, welding, painting, clothing warehouse. In order picking in Oldenburg, the voice system also works in English; you only need to know the numbers from 1 to 100 in German. Mechanics need German at B1 level.

How much can I earn?

Rates are from €15.33 to €19.00 per hour gross. On top of that, you get surcharges under the GVP collective agreement: nights +25%, Sundays +50%, public holidays +100%, overtime +25%. In order picking in Oldenburg, there is a performance bonus of a few hundred euros up to €1,000 a month or more.

Do I have to pay the agency?

No. For workers, placement through Office54 is free.

Do you help with accommodation?

Yes, if you need it. Accommodation costs €390 per person per month. For many jobs, we organise transport to work for €5 per working day, there and back.

Which cities are the jobs in?

Oldenburg, Hamburg and towns in Lower Saxony: Haselünne, Nordhorn, Werlte, Lingen, Hude, Westerstede.

Which languages can I speak with you?

English, German, Russian, Ukrainian, Polish or Romanian.

I am not from the EU. Can I contact you?

Yes, write to us. We will tell you which documents you need and help you with your residence permit.
