Office54 connects people and companies worldwide

We are Office54 GmbH, an experienced staffing agency founded in 2021. Our founder brings more than 20 years of industry experience. Our goal is clear: we create added value through tailored solutions in recruitment, temporary staffing and direct placement.

What makes us unique? Our multicultural orientation and global reach. With a team that speaks Ukrainian, Russian, English, Romanian, Polish and German, we specifically cover the Eastern European market and recruit skilled and unskilled workers from all over the world. This multilingualism allows us to integrate international talent quickly and efficiently.

Our strength lies in flexibly adapting to the needs of our clients and employees. We rely on satisfied partnerships because we know: our clients' success is also our success.

Our services for applicants

Office54 supports job seekers at every stage of their career. Our services include:

Diverse job opportunities: your entry into the German labour market

Discover exciting jobs in various industries to start or develop your career in Germany. As a direct employer, we give you access to positions that perfectly match your skills.

Employment with takeover: your chance of a permanent position

Show your potential and secure the chance to be taken on from a temporary position into a permanent one. Start a long-term career with us.

Direct placement: we find the job that suits you

We connect you directly with employers who value your qualifications. We place you in positions that match your career goals.

Office54 offers tailored staffing solutions that increase your company's productivity and efficiency. Our services for companies include:

Qualified staff

We offer access to a pool of reliable, qualified candidates who are carefully selected to meet your company's specific requirements.

Flexible solutions

From temporary to permanent placements, our staffing solutions adapt to your changing needs and offer flexibility in workforce management.

Time and cost efficiency

Thanks to our optimized hiring process, companies save valuable time and significantly reduce costs. Our efficient processes let you focus fully on your core tasks.

Why Office54 GmbH?

At Office54 GmbH, we offer both companies and applicants far more than classic staffing services. Here are four reasons why we are the ideal partner for your career and staffing needs:

Tailored all-in-one solutions

We offer specialized recruiting solutions for companies and attractive job offers for applicants, with a special focus on skilled workers and helpers from Eastern Europe. We help you find the right position or the right staff.

Multilingual all-round support

Our multilingual team supports companies and applicants in six languages: German, English, Russian, Ukrainian, Polish and Romanian. We accompany applicants from placement through to administrative matters such as residence permits, health insurance and bank accounts.

Accommodation and transport

On request, we offer applicants comfortable accommodation and daily transport to the workplace. This makes it easier to start the new job and ensures a stress-free journey to work.

In-house forklift school

Through our in-house forklift driver training, we give applicants the opportunity to expand their qualifications and improve their chances on the job market. Companies benefit from well-trained employees who are ready to work.
