Our mission

Our mission is to connect talented skilled and unskilled workers with suitable employers in Germany. We want to make it easier for qualified people from abroad to enter the German labour market while supporting companies looking for motivated workers.

What drives us?

Our mission is to simplify and improve the hiring experience for both job seekers and employers. We are dedicated to

  1. For job seekers

    We give applicants access to hand-picked job offers that match their qualifications and ambitions. Our goal is to give job seekers an easy way to find suitable positions and apply directly with us.

  2. For companies.

    Companies looking for qualified and motivated employees can contact us directly through our website. We help you find the best candidates for your open positions, efficiently and in a targeted way.

Our core goals

  • Hand-picked job offers

    We only present jobs that have been checked and approved by our team. Applicants can be sure they will only find relevant and serious job offers that match their skills and interests.

  • Easy contact for companies Companies can send us inquiries without any hassle to learn more about our services and available candidates. Our team is ready to offer tailored solutions for your staffing needs.
  • Support with integration We continue to support our applicants after a successful placement and help them settle into their new working environment. This includes help with organisational questions and support with integrating into everyday working life.
